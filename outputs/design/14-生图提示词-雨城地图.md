# 生图提示词：雨城前两关地图

这些提示词用于交给 GPT / Midjourney 等生图模型生成“可以放进游戏里的地图界面草稿”。

通用风格锚点：

```text
A stylized metroidvania area map UI. Dark navy/blue-grey parchment-like game map panel.
Rooms are irregular connected tiles drawn with thin light cyan/gold lines.
Small simplified icons: red boss skull, golden key/door, cyan ability gem, blue NPC dot, yellow player dot.
Dashed yellow lines show shortcuts/ability gates.
Title at top center, small legend at bottom right. Rain drops faintly visible inside rooms.
Use simplified Chinese labels for areas and room names.
Do not copy specific copyrighted game assets.
```

```text
Top-down game map for a rain-themed first district called "城南雨市街".

Layout order from left to right:
1. Top-left: "夜巡司天台" safe room with a small bench/rest marker.
2. A tall vertical room "雨帘长巷" below it, with three small roof ledges connected by jump arrows.
3. Central-left wide room "收摊水街": market stalls, puddles, a few breakable vases.
4. Upper room above the street: "摊位顶棚区", made of several tilted canopy platforms.
5. Center-right event room "炒粉灰影现场" connected to the street, marked with a red event star.
6. Lower room "老巡夜员亭": small NPC hut with a blue NPC dot.
7. Lower-right high platform "印章高台": golden ability gem "夜巡印章".
8. A hidden side room "水洼后巷" connected below the market with dashed path.
9. Bottom room "旧药铺": small secret room with plant decoration.
10. Far bottom-center boss chamber "排水渠：收伞人": red boss skull.
11. Far right large locked door room "老剧院侧门锁": golden lock and closed door.

Dashed yellow line from "印章高台" should visually connect back to "摊位顶棚区", showing a roof shortcut.
Solid cyan path connects main events. Add room borders with rounded corners.
Label every room in simplified Chinese. Show small event icons inside rooms.
Include legend at bottom right: 案件事件、NPC、能力、Boss、能力捷径、锁定门.
```

## 第二关提示词

```text
Top-down game map for a rain-themed second district called "旧剧院与封门车站".

Layout:
1. Left-middle entry room "剧院侧门", connected from previous map by an arrow labeled "第一关入口".
2. Upper-center large room "散场大厅": audience rows and a two-level balcony; jump arrows between seats and balcony.
3. Right-middle room "舞台池": stage with curtains and a red mid-boss marker "幕布幽灵".
4. Center room below the hall: "后台化妆室" with NPC "戏班主" and a golden key "戏票钥匙".
5. Right room "放映间/幕布室": projector and glowing screen, event star "案件二：幕布歌声".
6. Far right tall room "吊杆层": horizontal hanging rails like moving platforms; path continues upward.
7. Top-left outdoor room "雨钟台": bell tower with long rain trails and rooftop jump platforms; it visually looks down at the final seal target.
8. Lower-left room "寄存室": rows of lockers, one open locker glowing; event star "案件三：呼吸柜".
9. Center-bottom wide room "站台区": train platform on both sides of rail tracks.
10. Right-bottom boss chamber "末班车老板": giant bus/train silhouette, red boss marker.
11. Bottom-right sealed target "封印终点": golden pillar with a closed seal ring.
12. Bottom of map: "地下维修通道" connecting "化妆室" and "站台区", drawn as a dashed shortcut.

Rain should be visible in outdoor rooms only: 雨钟台 and 封印终点.
Indoor rooms have dark dry background. The final seal target is visible on the map from the beginning but marked with a lock.
Use simplified Chinese labels and a legend at bottom right.
```

## 使用建议

- 第一次先让 GPT 生成“黑白线稿地图版式”，不要直接上色。
- 确认房间连接与图标位置后，再生成“上色 UI 版”。
- 最后用同一提示词生成多个区域变体，保持画风一致。
- 不要直接要求复制空洞骑士/冥界清道夫的美术素材，只参考“地图界面排版逻辑”。
